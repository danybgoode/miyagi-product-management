# Seed — US operator needfinding and value-proposition validation

## Outcome — 2026-08-03

The desk-research baseline is complete and is sufficient to start the ordered US bets while
progressive interviews continue. It resolves the required segment, job, trigger, first behavior,
pilot structure, module proof, retained-system baseline, agent boundaries, customer language, and
disqualification rules. No app epic was scaffolded for this spike.

The first recruit is an owner-led operator managing 3–10 active shops for brands that make,
design, or meaningfully curate what they sell. The first behavior is submission of three shop URLs
for a no-cutover exit-readiness review. The pilot is one operator, three consenting shops, 90 days,
parallel operation, and a completed real-order lifecycle before any merchant-controlled cutover.

Interviews remain required as a continuous evidence system. They can reverse provisional decisions
using the explicit triggers in the outcome brief; they do not gate recruiting, grooming, or
generalizable US-readiness work.

## Approved direction

The first US buyer is an agency/operator managing multiple client shops. The target specialization
is operators serving distinctive independent-product merchants, not commodity dropshippers.

## Research source

Run the agency program in:

`madmen/clients/miyagi-sanchez/us-operator-gtm-discovery-plan.md`

Minimum sample: 6 operators, 3–4 merchants, and 2 curated marketplace/community founders. Include
Shopify and WooCommerce, one meaningful multichannel operator, one Veeqo/ShipStation user, and
non-AI-enthusiasts.

## Decisions required before product grooming

- exact operator subsegment and buying trigger;
- leading job/pain and first desired behavior;
- pilot offer/pricing and three-shop access;
- which Miyagi Channels, Ledger, and Dispatch capability is the leading proof;
- integrations that must remain;
- acceptable agent autonomy/confirmation boundaries;
- language customers understand for “original commerce”;
- disqualification rules.

## Exit artifact

Evidence-backed persona, value-proposition canvas, ranked problem ledger, three-shop pilot candidate,
message hierarchy, and product-scope recommendation. This is agency research; do not scaffold an app
epic merely to mark the interviews complete.

# Seed — US owned-shop positioning and proof launch

## Purpose

Convert a passed US operator pilot into a digital GTM system without implying a US marketplace or
unproven self-service scale.

## Entry gate

- three-shop partner pilot passed;
- operator persona/value proposition validated beyond the pilot;
- case-study permissions recorded;
- partner-led and self-service proof labeled separately;
- module parity claims tied to a versioned evidence matrix;
- support, shipping, payment, returns, and operating limitations disclosed.

## Likely scope

Operator landing page, pilot case study, qualifying application, founder-led outbound system,
community/partner content, and measurement plan. Paid acquisition is a later optimization after one
message converts organically.

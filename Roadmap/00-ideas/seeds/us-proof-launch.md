# Pitch — US owned-shop positioning and proof launch

> **⚠️ ARCHIVED 2026-08-10.** Its epic is archived. There is no pilot to publish a proof of; `/us`
> ships as the finished marketplace. See `Roadmap/07-agentic-and-federated-commerce/us-marketplace/`.

## The ask

Turn a passed three-shop US operator pilot into one honest, proof-led digital launch that helps the
next qualified operator apply with three real shops — without implying an open US marketplace,
proven self-service scale, universal platform parity, or outcomes the pilot did not observe.

## Appetite, class, and lane

- **Appetite:** **S** — one proof/claim-lock sprint and one launch/learning sprint. This budget buys a
  versioned public evidence matrix, one factual case study on the existing `/us` surface, reuse of
  the `#US-2` application, a bounded founder-led distribution packet, and a 30-day decision record.
  It does not buy a new funnel, CRM, case-study CMS, outreach automation, or paid acquisition.
- **Class / archetype:** **Feature / Grower** — the capability is proof-backed acquisition, and its
  acceptance ends in a qualified-behavior signal rather than “the page exists.”
- **Lane:** **Shaped bet** — the next acquisition motion is strategic, but the solution is deliberately
  constrained to proof and distribution over already-planned platform rails.
- **Risk:** **LOW** — public copy, a versioned read-only proof artifact, analytics allowlists, and
  founder-owned launch materials. If implementation discovers a database, auth, permission, commerce,
  money, or fulfillment change, stop and re-groom that work as HIGH rather than absorbing it here.

## Problem

The research baseline already defines the operator, job, first behavior, message hierarchy, and
no-cutover offer. `#US-2` already owns the professional recruitment surface, structured three-shop
application, review/activation lifecycle, and privacy-safe funnel events. `#US-3` is already scoped to
produce the only evidence that can make a later launch credible: three consented shops, parallel
operation, a delivered USD order, actual payment/label/economic evidence, limitations, and an explicit
continue/reshape/stop decision.

The remaining problem is therefore not “build a US landing page.” It is to convert those observed
results into claims a buyer can inspect and trust, disclose exactly what remains partner-led or retained
outside Miyagi, and route a bounded organic audience into the application `#US-2` already provides.

Without that claim discipline, a conventional launch would overstate a one-operator proof as a US
marketplace or self-service product. Without distribution and a decision window, a case study becomes a
poster rather than a growth instrument.

## Entry gate — scope may be approved now; execution stays blocked

No build, publication, or outbound launch starts until all six conditions are true:

1. `#US-2 · Miyagi Partners proposition and recruiting portal v3` is shipped, enabled for the founding
   operator track, and its three-shop application/review path has passed the product-owner smoke.
2. `#US-3 · US operator commerce pilot` is `status: shipped` and its close decision is **continue** or
   an explicitly accepted **reshape** that still supports an honest owned-shop proof. A **stop** outcome
   archives this seed; it does not trigger a softer marketing claim.
3. The `#US-3` proof includes one observed real USD order through the agreed payment, tax, label,
   tracking, delivery, reconciliation, and economic-evidence loop. Readiness screenshots or test-mode
   activity alone do not satisfy the gate.
4. The continuous evidence review includes a post-baseline set of three qualified conversations, with
   at least one qualified operator outside the pilot, and records whether the operator persona, leading
   job, and promise held or changed.
5. Publication permission is recorded separately for the operator and each merchant as
   **attributed**, **anonymized**, or **not publishable**. Application consent, discovery access,
   pilot operation, real-order permission, and case-study permission remain distinct decisions.
6. The product owner approves the public-safe evidence matrix, material-connection disclosure, support
   promise, operating limits, and every attributed quote/logo/name before publication.

If any required source is unavailable, record it as unavailable and stop. Do not turn missing evidence
into “none,” zero, or an implied pass.

## Outcome and signal

After this ships, a qualified operator can land on `/us`, inspect what Miyagi actually proved and did
not prove, understand the partner-led support model, and submit exactly three shops through the existing
application. The product owner can trace a qualified submission back to a coarse organic source and the
proof version without putting shop URLs, contact data, or free text into analytics.

The first launch cycle is successful when one **screening-passed operator application containing three
real shop URLs** comes from the bounded organic wave within 30 days. Pageviews, generic email signups,
unqualified applications, and direct-merchant interest do not substitute for that behavior. At day 30,
record **continue, reshape, or stop** before considering paid acquisition.

## Stage-2.5 bucket

## Public proof and publication contract

Maintain two artifacts with different audiences:

- The **private evidence index** remains the `#US-3` proof bundle. It may contain restricted provider,
  order, support, and operational references. It never ships to the public app or analytics.
- The **public-safe proof matrix** is a versioned, dependency-free frontend artifact consumed by the
  `/us` render and its claim specs. It contains no secrets, customer PII, private shop URLs, provider
  credentials, or source documents.

Each public matrix version records:

- `proof_version`, observation window, reviewer, and last-reviewed date;
- cohort shape and support mode, with the literal denominator (`one operator / three shops`);
- one row per claimed capability: partner access, Shopify import, native setup, owned-shop catalog,
  checkout/payment/tax, shipping/label/tracking, reconciliation, and operating economics;
- for each row: **observed**, **supported but not observed**, **retained external**, or **unavailable**;
- whether the evidence was **partner-led** or **self-service** — unaided self-service stays “not
  tested” until separately observed;
- the public claim, limitation, private evidence reference, permission mode, and approving reviewer;
- the systems and responsibilities retained outside Miyagi: incumbent channel, support, payments,
  tax, shipping, returns/refunds, accounting, email/CRM, analytics, reviews/SEO, or anything else the
  pilot kept.

Publication rules:

- A factual capability row can publish only when its evidence and reviewer are present. Missing evidence
  cannot default to observed.
- Names, logos, direct quotes, screenshots, and identifiable shop details follow the participant's most
  restrictive current permission. Revocation removes the attributed presentation without rewriting the
  underlying factual matrix.
- The page states that founding participants received a no-platform-fee proof and discloses any other
  material connection beside the case study, not in a distant policy page.
- Results are framed as this cohort's observed experience. No “typical,” percentage-improvement, scale,
  savings, revenue, conversion, or parity claim appears unless separately evidenced and approved.
- “Miyagi operated in Mexico before this pilot” and “the pilot observed US owned-shop commerce” remain
  separate claims. The page never implies a US marketplace, marketplace demand, self-service launch, or
  a general replacement for retained systems.
- No fabricated testimonial, composite quote, logo wall, application count, waitlist count, or customer
  story is allowed.

  permission record allows them, otherwise publish an honest anonymized cohort account.
- The flag-on `#US-2` `/us` experience changes from “founding hypothesis/recruitment” to “observed proof +
  next operator invitation.” The flag-off invitation and structural no-catalog boundary remain intact.
- One primary CTA into the existing `#US-2` three-shop operator application. Do not fork validation,
  review, activation, consent, identity, or partner-grant semantics.
- English-first operator copy, precise Mexico-versus-US language, the complete support/operating limits,
  and a clear “not a fit” list.
- Server-rendered/no-JavaScript proof content with current metadata and one agent-readable pointer through
  the existing marketing/about discovery rail; no new MCP tool or commerce capability.
- A founder-led launch packet built from the observed matrix: personalized operator outreach, one merchant
  variant, one warm-introduction note, one community/partner introduction, and one founder post.

  analytics where they may contain sensitive text.

  checkout, payment, tax, shipping, returns, fulfillment, or ledger capability.
- A new application, CRM, leads table, partner identity, consent ledger, grant role, admin queue, or
  case-study CMS. Those are `#US-2`/`#US-3` dependencies, not work to duplicate here.
- Claims that `#US-2` or `#US-3` did not actually ship and observe.
- Automated cold email, bulk messaging, scraped lists, contact enrichment, lead scoring, autonomous
  follow-up, partner-directory work, or product-sent community messages.
- Paid search/social, retargeting, affiliate economics, referral commissions, certification, badges,
  operator pricing, revenue share, exclusivity, lead volume, or future-income promises.
- Public customer data, exact revenue, private order/provider evidence, secrets, credentials, or
  identifiable screenshots without explicit current permission.
- An externally claimed “Shopify killer,” “all-in-one,” “fully autonomous,” “US marketplace,” universal
  parity, accounting/tax truth, or self-service proposition.
- A broad bilingual expansion or rewrite of the live Mexico Promotor funnel.

## Rabbit holes — decisions made here

- **Do not rebuild `#US-2`.** It already owns `/us`, the structured application, track-aware review,
  partner activation, and funnel events. If that dependency ships differently, reshape this seed against
  its actual contracts before scaffolding rather than recreating them.
- **One URL, not a case-study site.** Keep proof and invitation on `/us`. Adding `/us/*` content routes is
  unnecessary and weakens the deliberately simple “US invitation, no catalog subtree” architecture.
- **Code-backed proof, not editable marketing copy.** The public matrix is versioned and spec-guarded.
  Do not route evidence claims through generic CMS overrides where a copy edit could detach a claim from
  its evidence, limitation, permission, or reviewer.
- **Facts before quotes.** The case study is useful without a testimonial. If nobody grants quote/logo/name
  permission, publish the factual, anonymized proof or do not publish; never manufacture social proof.
- **One pilot is not a benchmark.** Show the denominator and observed boundary. Do not calculate “average,”
  improvement, or typical result from the three shops unless the underlying comparison was deliberately
  measured and the resulting claim is approved.
- **Partner-led and self-service are separate.** A founder/operator-assisted result never supports an
  unaided activation claim. The matrix makes the distinction per capability, not in a footnote.
- **The existing flag is enough.** `partners.recruiting_v3_enabled` already gates the new `/us` and
  application behavior. No second launch flag is added; flag-off or a deploy revert is the rollback.
- **Outbound stays human.** The product may supply proof-linked copy and attributed URLs, but it does not
  send, scrape, personalize, or follow up. The product owner verifies every recipient and permission.
- **Organic before paid.** A failed organic wave is a message/offer/proof signal to investigate, not
  permission to buy traffic.

## Sprint and story map

### Sprint 1 — Lock and publish the observed proof

#### Story 1.1 — Version the public proof and case-study contract — risk: LOW

**As a prospective operator, I want** every claim tied to an observed result and explicit limitation
**so that** I can distinguish evidence from launch copy.

Acceptance:

- One public-safe matrix implements the status, support-mode, permission, reviewer, and retained-system
  contract above and identifies its private `#US-3` evidence references without exposing them publicly.
- The product owner approves the exact cohort denominator, material-connection disclosure, attribution
  mode, limitations, and support promise.
- A pure spec refuses a claim with missing evidence/reviewer/limitation, an unapproved attribution, an
  unknown status, mixed self-service/partner-led wording, or a secret/PII-shaped public field.
- The spec is observed red through a deliberate broken-matrix mutation before restoration.

QA: dependency-free proof-schema/claim-policy spec plus product-owner comparison to the private evidence
index. No browser smoke in this story.

#### Story 1.2 — Turn `/us` from hypothesis into proof-led invitation — risk: LOW

**As a qualified operator, I want** to inspect the pilot result, boundaries, and next-cohort offer
**so that** I can decide whether submitting three real shops is worth the trust cost.

Acceptance:

- With the existing `#US-2` flag on, `/us` renders the observed result, cohort denominator, case study,
  capability/limitation matrix, partner-led versus self-service boundary, retained systems, support model,
  fit/not-fit criteria, material connection, and one primary application CTA.
- The CTA enters the shipped `#US-2` application; no parallel form, table, review, identity, consent, or
  grant path is created.
- Flag off preserves the earlier honest invitation. `/us/l/<anything>` remains a structural 404, and no

  and is legible on mobile/desktop. Metadata and the existing agent/about discovery rail point to the same

QA: API/SSR claim-and-link spec, flag-state negation, structural catalog-404 regression, `tsc`, lint,
build, and an anonymous `live-smoke` desktop/mobile pass on the preview. The product owner checks any
attributed name/logo/quote and real social preview.

### Sprint 2 — Run one organic launch cycle and decide

#### Story 2.1 — Produce the permission-safe founder launch packet — risk: LOW

**As the product owner, I want** proof-linked messages for a bounded, human-sent cohort **so that** I can
test the proposition without an automated campaign or unsupported claim drift.

Acceptance:

- The existing outreach source is revised into five complete, send-ready variants: operator, merchant,
  warm introduction, community/partner introduction, and founder post.
- Every proof claim and quote points to the current public matrix version; every bracket is resolved before
  a send; no recipient belief, consent, dissatisfaction, revenue, or client authority is inferred.
- One coarse source/message identifier and proof-version UTM pattern is fixed. No contact or shop data is
  encoded in links or analytics.
- The owner approves 12–15 individual contacts and at most two community/partner placements, sends from a
  named human, and stops after two follow-ups or immediately on opt-out.

QA: proof-version/link sweep, unresolved-placeholder guard, claim allowlist comparison, and owner review of
the real recipient/permission sheet. Sending/posting is product-owner-owned and not automated by a builder.

#### Story 2.2 — Measure qualified commitment and close the 30-day launch decision — risk: LOW

**As the product owner, I want** the organic wave measured through a screening-passed three-shop
application **so that** I know whether to continue, reshape, or stop before buying traffic.

Acceptance:

- Existing `#US-2` funnel events accept only the fixed proof version, track/stage, coarse source, and
  message variant; URLs, contact/company fields, free text, evidence, and merchant data are rejected or
  dropped.
- The scorecard reports known-present, known-absent, and unavailable separately across the agreed funnel.
  No unavailable query becomes a zero or a “no applications” conclusion.
- At day 30, the owner records the bounded sends/placements, screening-passed three-shop applications,
  disqualification/objection themes, support load, and a continue/reshape/stop decision.
- Paid acquisition remains blocked unless the organic wave produces the agreed qualified application and
  the owner separately approves a later optimization bet.

QA: event allowlist/privacy spec, three-state scorecard fixtures, deliberate-red mutation, and owner
comparison to the application queue. No additional browser smoke beyond Sprint 1.

## UX heuristics and rails check

- **Hierarchical task analysis:** proof first → limitations/support → fit/not-fit → submit three shops.
  Architecture/module detail stays secondary to the operator's job.
- **Progressive disclosure:** one observed headline and denominator lead; capability evidence and retained
  systems expand below it; private evidence IDs never render.
- **Recognition over recall:** every claim shows proof version, observation boundary, support mode, and
  limitation beside the claim.
- **Error prevention:** no generic “apply” CTA if the `#US-2` dependency is unavailable; no attributed
  material without current permission; no self-service inference from partner-led work.
- **Recovery:** permission revocation removes attribution; dependency unavailability returns the honest
  invitation/application-unavailable state; neither becomes an empty or false success.
- **Agent readability:** substantive SSR HTML and one existing marketing/about pointer; no new MCP tool.
- **CI guards covering this surface:** design-token/raw-color guard, marketing readability/metadata checks,
  application/API contract from `#US-2`, analytics privacy allowlist, new proof-policy spec, TypeScript,
  lint, build, and browser smoke.
- **Audits-lens findings that apply:** the June refresh contains no direct US-proof-launch finding. Apply
  its reuse-first, claim-honesty, and seller-acquisition trust discipline; `/us` is a platform surface, so
  the white-label channel-parity finding does not create extra render targets.
- **Design-language debt:** the current `/us` page is bespoke but already tokenized and static. Reuse its
  shell and the shipped `/vende` primitives; do not introduce a US-only component language or put evidence
  claims into editable copy overrides.

## Current research and external constraints

- The approved product baseline remains the 2026-08-03 US operator needfinding outcome, recruitment-page

  cohort receives a no-platform-fee founding proof, the launch must disclose that connection and publish
  only actual, permissioned experience:

- This scope sets product safeguards, not legal advice. The product owner obtains any required legal review
  of testimonial, trademark/comparative, privacy, and consent copy before publication.

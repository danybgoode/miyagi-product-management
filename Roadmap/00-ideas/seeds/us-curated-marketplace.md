# Pitch — US curated marketplace: original independent sellers

## The ask

**As a US buyer, I want** one marketplace collection of transaction-ready goods from independent
brands whose authorship, production relationship, and operating readiness have been reviewed **so
that** I can discover something distinctive without doing counterfeit, dropshipping, or provenance
forensics myself.

This opens a **Founding US Collection**, not a general US marketplace. It turns the US owned-shop
proof into carefully admitted cross-shop discovery while keeping every checkout, order, payment,
fulfillment, support, and return relationship seller-specific.

## Appetite, class, and lane

- **Appetite:** M — enough for one human-operated admission system, one US marketplace Sales Channel,
  buyer and agent discovery, provenance/trust presentation, issue escalation, and a controlled
  opening. It does not buy automated moderation, a universal protection program, or broad category
  expansion.
- **Class / archetype:** Feature / Builder.
- **Lane:** shaped bet. `priority: wave-3` records sequence; `underwritten_by: null` means no current
  wave file has funded it. This checkout contains no `Roadmap/bets/` directory, so priority is not
  treated as underwriting evidence.
- **Risk:** HIGH — the work adds a migration-backed admission boundary, a live Sales Channel,
  marketplace catalog exposure, seller suspension, and a path into US checkout.

## Problem

Miyagi now knows that `us` is a real operating market, but it intentionally means **invitation**, not
marketplace. The platform has no US marketplace Sales Channel, `/us/l/*` is structurally absent, and
UCP/MCP must return a named unavailable state rather than Mexico rows. `#US-3` is scoped to prove
three US **owned shops** and explicitly creates no marketplace. `#US-4` is scoped to publish only
that observed proof and invite the next operator.

Opening the current gates without a separate admission system would make every technically buyable US
listing discoverable, confuse owned-shop readiness with marketplace qualification, and create a
generic supply flood that contradicts the buyer promise. Conversely, building a new order, payment,
shipping, shop, or catalog model would duplicate the US commerce rails the ordered dependencies are
already funding.

The missing product is therefore narrow but real: a durable human decision about **which shop may
join**, a separate decision about **which listing may appear**, and one controlled way to project
approved products into Medusa's US marketplace Sales Channel.

## Product decision — approved 2026-08-03

### Buyer promise and assortment

- Launch one **Founding US Collection** of tangible, shippable, non-regulated consumer goods from
  independent brands that make, design, or meaningfully curate what they sell.
- The public promise is: **distinctive goods from independent brands, with visible authorship and
  production disclosure**. “Original commerce” remains internal shorthand.
- The final lead theme and complementary assortment come from qualified supply interviews; the build
  does not invent a category before the candidates exist.
- Food, supplements, cosmetics with regulated claims, weapons, controlled goods, recalled goods,
  hazardous goods, services, rentals, subscriptions, and event admission are outside the first
  collection. A later compliance decision may admit a category; silence never does.

### Dual admission gate

- **Shop gate:** a human reviewer approves the seller's authorship/control model, production-partner
  disclosure, identity/operating readiness, support ownership, and policy fit.
- **Listing gate:** each product has its own evidence and decision. Shop approval never bulk-publishes
  a catalog, and one approved product never proves a sibling product.
- **Publication truth:** Medusa Sales Channel membership remains the only commerce fact meaning “this
  product is published to the US marketplace.” The admission store authorizes a reviewed transition;
  it never mirrors or replaces channel membership.
- Sellers/operators may submit evidence and appeal, but never approve themselves. v1 supports one
  documented appeal. Revalidation occurs annually and after a material authorship, production,
  ownership, safety, or fulfillment change.

### Operating contract

- The participating merchant remains seller of record under the counsel-approved `#US-3` contract.
  Marketplace checkout keeps the same direct-charge, tax, fulfillment, and support responsibility.
- One seller per cart and order. No cross-seller or cross-country mixed cart.
- The seller fulfills and supports the order under a clearly disclosed policy. Miyagi supplies a
  visible issue-report path, an auditable escalation queue, and suspension; v1 does not promise
  purchase protection, a universal return window, or platform-funded refunds.
- Counsel must approve the INFORM Consumers Act, marketplace-facilitator/tax, product-safety,
  shipping/refund, privacy, and seller-of-record responsibility map before opening.

## Outcome and signal

After this ships, a buyer can enter `/us`, understand the Founding US Collection promise, browse only
approved US listings, inspect who made/designed/curated the item and any disclosed production partner,
buy one listing through its seller's proven US rails, contact the seller, and report suspicious
activity. A buyer or agent asking for `market=us` receives the same admitted catalog and never Mexico
rows.

The product owner can accept the opening by proving all of these in one observed run:

1. an unapproved shop and an approved shop with an unapproved listing are both absent from web and
   agent discovery;
2. an approved listing appears with its evidence-backed provenance and seller responsibility;
3. one real USD marketplace order completes through the same seller-specific payment, tax,
   fulfillment, support, and refund seams proven by `#US-3`;
4. disabling the marketplace stops new discovery/cart/payment entry without breaking owned-shop
   reads or the recovery actions for an already-paid order;
5. a buyer report reaches the review queue, and suspension removes future marketplace discovery
   without deleting the seller's owned shop or commerce history.

### New primitive — bounded deliberately

Add a market-neutral non-commerce admission store in Supabase, provisionally
`marketplace_admissions` plus append-only `marketplace_admission_events`, keyed to canonical Medusa
seller/product IDs and `market_code`. It stores evidence references, decision/reason, reviewer,
appeal/revalidation state, and compliance-status references—**never** product price/inventory/channel
membership, bank details, tax IDs, or raw identity documents. Medusa remains the commerce system of
record; designated providers retain sensitive verification data where counsel permits.

The architect must validate this shape against the live `#US-2/#US-3` schema before locking it. If a
shipped, correctly bounded admission primitive exists by build time, cite and extend that source
instead of creating these tables.

## Rabbit holes — decisions made here

- **Admission is not publication.** Supabase records the human policy decision; Medusa Sales Channel
  membership records marketplace publication. The apply workflow reads an approved current decision,
  proves seller/product identity, then mutates membership and re-reads it. It never writes the mirror
  first or reports success from an unchanged row.
- **Shop approval is necessary, never sufficient.** Every listing gets its own evidence and decision.
  A catalog-wide “approve all” action is outside v1.
- **The marketplace channel does not carry buyability.** `#US-3`'s US operating channel remains the
  cart/checkout channel. Do not link its publishable key to both channels or make callers choose a
  channel—the owned-shop epic already disproved that shape. The marketplace channel is the discovery
  filter only.
- **Validate before every write.** Flag, current admission, seller/product ownership, operating
  readiness, price/currency, allowed product class, and channel/resource resolution are checked before
  membership changes. A partial apply is a non-2xx status, not a green body with warnings.
- **Revocation has two responsibilities.** Remove future marketplace discovery immediately; preserve
  the recovery actions for paid/in-flight orders. Disabling discovery must never strand fulfillment,
  refund, tracking, or buyer contact.
- **Do not store sensitive compliance documents casually.** The workflow keeps provider references and
  verification outcomes, not raw bank/tax/identity material, unless the counsel-approved security
  design explicitly requires otherwise.
- **Structural absence becomes an explicit contract.** Adding `/us/l/*` removes the strongest existing
  fail-closed boundary. One tested market-availability resolver must replace it across page, API, UCP,
  MCP, sitemap, metadata, cart, and checkout populations.
- **The assortment threshold is an operational gate, not homepage copy.** Counts include only approved,
  priced, in-stock, transaction-ready listings whose sellers pass the complete operating contract.
  Unknown/unavailable reads never count as zero or as ready.
- **Originality is not a legal warranty.** Public copy says what evidence was reviewed and what the
  seller disclosed; it does not claim Miyagi proved copyright, authenticity, safety, or universal
  ethical production.

## UX heuristics and rails check

## Kill switch / runtime gate

**Recommendation approved as part of this pitch:** Golden-managed enablement flag
`markets.us_marketplace_enabled`.

- **Polarity:** enablement, default `false`; create it **DISABLED in every environment** before any
  caller reads it. A code catalog entry without the Golden definition is not a switch.
- **Seam:** one server-owned market-availability resolver composes registry, resource, flag, and readiness
  state for catalog/discovery/cart/payment entry. Admission and authorization remain their own mandatory
  checks because flags fail open and are never security boundaries.
- **Mechanism:** the current Golden feature-flag catalog/control plane. The route tree does not rely on an
  Edge-only flag SDK; server-rendered surfaces and APIs consume the same resolver contract.
- **Rollback carve-through:** OFF prevents new marketplace exposure and new marketplace cart/payment
  entry, but preserves US owned shops and the minimum fulfillment, tracking, buyer-contact, refund, and
  dispute actions required for already-paid orders.

## Current research and external constraints

- Etsy's current Creativity Standards provide a useful vocabulary—made, designed, handpicked, sourced—
  and require production-partner disclosure and original finished-product media for relevant classes.
  Miyagi adopts the evidence principles, not Etsy's full category breadth or policy claims:

- The FTC says the INFORM Consumers Act can cover smaller niche marketplaces and requires covered

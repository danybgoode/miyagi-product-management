# Seed — US curated marketplace

## Thesis

Build discovery around sought-after original independent sellers, not an open flood of generic
catalog supply. The scarce input is credible, distinctive supply buyers can trust without forensic
work.

## Working eligibility standard

Authorship/control, distinctiveness, provenance, disclosure, original media, operational
reliability, and no undisclosed dropshipping/counterfeit/copied-design arbitrage. Production
partners and print-on-demand may qualify when the seller owns the design and discloses the
relationship.

## Entry gate

- US owned-shop rails proven;
- Originality Standard has evidence rubric, reviewer workflow, appeal, and revalidation;
- working planning threshold of 15–25 vetted shops and 150 transaction-ready listings is validated
  through supply interviews;
- concentrated assortment and buyer promise chosen;
- US marketplace-specific fulfillment, support, returns, and acquisition plan approved;
- no cross-country mixed carts in v1.

## Do not scaffold yet

A route, waitlist, or aggregate listing count is not marketplace readiness.

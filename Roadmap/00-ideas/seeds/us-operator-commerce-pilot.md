# Seed — US operator commerce pilot

## Approved proof bar

One agency/operator activates three consenting, qualified original-product client shops through one
partner relationship:

- one catalog imported from an existing platform;
- one shop created through the native Miyagi setup path;
- all three with live owned-shop surface, real catalog, USD pricing, payments, and US shipping
  ready;
- at least one real USD order paid, labeled, tracked, delivered, and represented in the economic
  view;
- one scoped partner identity operates all three with no cross-shop authorization leak.

Self-service shops are measured separately and do not satisfy this partner proof.

## Dependencies

- `market-architecture-foundation` shipped;
- `us-operator-needfinding` evidence and pilot operator secured;
- US legal/tax/payment/shipping assumptions reviewed;
- exact minimum Miyagi Channels, Ledger, and Dispatch jobs chosen from interviews.

## Working module lanes

- **Miyagi Partners:** multi-client control and evidence;
- **Miyagi Channels:** only the external channels the pilot actually needs;
- **Miyagi Ledger:** operating economics, not a QuickBooks replacement;
- **Miyagi Dispatch:** staged US rates/labels/tracking workflow, with parity defined against real
  Veeqo/ShipStation tasks.

## Not ready for deep grooming

Do not scaffold until the operator, three candidate shops, required channels, retained accounting
system, shipping provider shortlist, and pilot acceptance workflow are known.

# Seed — Miyagi Partners proposition and recruiting portal v3

## Approved direction

**Miyagi Partners** becomes the professional umbrella. **Promotor** is its accessible entry track,
not the whole identity. The portal should recruit a trusted commerce operator and make role
progression, tools, standards, support, and economics legible.

## Reuse

- shipped promoter application, handbook, offer/pricing, close, commission, and transfer rails;
- shipped multi-shop partner credential/grants/dashboard;
- shipped partner stewardship portfolio and activation scorecard;
- Shopify Partner recruitment logic as benchmark, not its scale claims.

## Scope gate

MADMEN must first deliver the program/brand/copy brief. Then groom the smallest portal epic against
the live promoter/partner contracts. Do not invent recurring economics, certification, counts,
income claims, or partner outcomes before operations approve and prove them.

## First behavior

An applicant identifies the correct track and submits a qualified application. Existing promoters
retain their operating paths during the brand transition.

# Raw seed — Affinity marketplace infrastructure

## Opportunity

Let a values-led, community, association, local, or editorial marketplace own its brand, seller
relationships, standards, and operations while Miyagi supplies commerce, synchronization,
operator, and agent infrastructure.

Little Blue Market is the current strategic archetype: vetted supply, clear values/community, and
documented synchronization from existing seller platforms.

## Sequence

1. Prove Miyagi's three-shop US operator pilot.
2. Map existing white-label, multi-tenant, migration, sync, partner, and marketplace capabilities.
3. Test a small integration/infrastructure partnership with one operator.
4. Calculate merchant migration/sync, operations, revenue, data, governance, and brand synergies.
5. Consider a deeper commercial combination only after the integration evidence.

## Boundary

Do not initiate a merger-first proposition or build generic marketplace SaaS from this seed. Deep
grooming requires a real partner, consent to evaluate, and an agreed operating/economic problem.
